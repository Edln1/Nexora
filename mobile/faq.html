A few things worth knowing.

Answers about MI-1, Nexora and our first launch.

What is MI-1?

MI-1 is Nexora’s first product launch: an interactive cat-play robot based on the Wellturn MI-1. It combines an expression display, movement, offline voice commands and automatic or remote-controlled play.

Does it have conversational AI or a phone app?

We do not offer MI-1 as a conversational AI assistant. Offline English and Chinese commands are supported; open-ended conversation, phone-app control and camera monitoring are not confirmed features.

Can I order now?

You can choose a finish and save MI-1 in your basket. Paid checkout is not open yet. Adding it to your basket does not place an order or reserve stock.

How much is MI-1?

The launch price is US$99 per robot. Shipping and taxes are not included in the basket estimate. Final costs will be shown before payment once ordering opens.

Where do you ship, and how long does delivery take?

Launch destinations and delivery times have not been announced. These will be published before orders open.

What is the return policy?

Return and warranty terms will be available before checkout opens. No paid orders are currently being accepted.

Are the lifestyle images photographs of the product in use?

The lifestyle scenes are AI-generated illustrations based on supplier imagery. Manufacturer product photographs are identified in the gallery.

Are the other Nexora products available?

MI-1 is our first launch. Other products shown in the collection are previews; their individual pages describe their status.
