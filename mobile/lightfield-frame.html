A CLEARER PICTURE

Good questions.

Does it project a hologram into the room?+

No. The depth effect is produced within a multiview screen. Manufacturer artwork may illustrate content protruding from the frame, but that is not free-space projection.

Can I use my own photographs?+

The manufacturer offers cloud conversion of photos into depth content. We are confirming the final upload process, account requirements and any conversion fees.

Can several people see the same effect?+

The listed 49 views describe the display’s viewing perspectives, not a guarantee of 49 viewers. The viewing zone and content determine the experience; we have not independently tested it.

Is the metal version the same?+

No. The supplier lists separate wood and metal configurations with different electronics, inputs and power. The specifications on this page refer to the wood version.

When can I order?+

This is a collection preview. Price, final configuration, warranty and delivery to the US and Canada will be published before ordering opens.
