Your marketing privacy choices

With your permission, Nexora uses the Meta Pixel to measure visits, product views, basket additions, checkout starts and confirmed purchases. These events help us understand which Facebook and Instagram ads lead to sales.

Meta receives browser and device information, visited page URLs and event details such as product identifiers, currency and order value. We do not include your name, email, shipping address or Stripe checkout token in the event data we send. Meta may associate events with your Meta account or browser identifiers.

Declining marketing cookies does not prevent shopping or payment. You can change your choice using the Cookie choices button on our MI-1 and checkout pages. We respect the browser’s Global Privacy Control signal.

Stripe processes payments and keeps the payment and delivery details needed for your order separately from advertising measurement. Browser tracking can be blocked or declined; Stripe payment records remain the source of truth for paid orders.
