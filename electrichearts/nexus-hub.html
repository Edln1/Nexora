BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A few things worth knowing.

Will it work with my devices?+

Compatibility depends on the exact hub, device model, protocol, and software. Review the confirmed compatibility list on the product page before buying.

Does a hub need an internet connection?+

Some systems support local controls; others rely on cloud services. Offline behavior varies by product and integration. Check the specific product documentation.

Are there subscriptions or extra accessories?+

Requirements vary. Check the listing for included accessories, optional services, subscription costs, and any extra bridges or adapters.
