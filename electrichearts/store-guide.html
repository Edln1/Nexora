02 / Charging, explained

Two kinds of wireless.
One useful distinction.

Bluetooth connects your phone’s audio to a speaker. Wireless phone charging is a separate function: the phone sits on a charging surface to receive power.

How PIXO’s phone-charging pad works

PIXO’s top pad supports compatible phones with up to 15 W wireless output. It needs a suitable external power adapter connected. The internal speaker battery supports portable listening; phone charging requires external power.

The listed box includes the N73 speaker, a charging cable and a manual. A wall adapter is not listed. Check your phone’s wireless-charging compatibility before ordering.

Do these robot speakers talk to an AI assistant?

The models in this guide are sold for the Bluetooth listening, display, clock, light or movement features described on their pages. Conversational AI is not advertised for these offered configurations. ORBI is specifically the M20 Bluetooth model. A character face alone does not indicate an AI assistant.

Can I upload custom pixel art to PIXO?

PIXO N73 includes built-in emoji and music-rhythm display effects. A custom-art app or image-upload feature is not documented for this model, so we do not advertise it. See the PIXO feature guide for the supported functions.

Which model has a phone charger and an alarm clock?

PIXO N73 combines Bluetooth sound, a clock with two alarms and a top wireless phone-charging pad. Phone charging requires a compatible phone and suitable external power. ORBI M20 has a clock, alarm and night light; a phone-charging pad is not part of its listed features.

Where do these products ship, and how long does delivery take?

Current checkout accepts Canadian addresses. Standard Canadian shipping is included in the displayed prices. Products ship from overseas fulfillment partners. Estimated delivery is 10–30 calendar days for PIXO, 15–45 for ECHO and 15–40 for ORBI, BOP and BT369. See the delivery policy before ordering.

Can I return a product?

Contact us within 30 days of delivery for a damaged, defective or incorrect product; we arrange a replacement or refund and cover required return shipping. For a change-of-mind return, the product must be unused and in its original packaging, and you pay return shipping. Contact us before returning an item and read the full returns policy.
