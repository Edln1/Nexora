Get to know MI-1.

What it does, what to expect and how to explore your new companion.

Expression and interaction.

A 1.83-inch display gives MI-1 its expressive face. Offline English and Chinese voice commands provide defined interactions; this is not open-ended AI conversation.

Play, your way.

Choose automatic play or use the remote control. Movement, sound effects and feather play add variety to supervised time with your cat. Infrared obstacle sensing is part of the hardware specification.

Choose a finish.

The selection page lets you compare black and white manufacturer images. The lifestyle gallery illustrates the black model. Select your finish and quantity before adding MI-1 to your basket.

Before the first play.

Use the manufacturer’s supplied manual for charging, setup and operating instructions. MI-1 is a cat-play product. Supervise play, check attachments before use and keep the play area away from stairs and exposed edges.

This guide is an overview, not a replacement for the product manual. We’ll add the matching setup manual when the launch configuration is confirmed.
