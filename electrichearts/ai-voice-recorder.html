GOOD TO KNOW

A few clear answers.

Is this a robot?+

It is a slim voice recorder. The twin circular controls give it a distinctive, almost robotic character, but it has no moving body or built-in face display.

Can it record without a phone?+

The supplier describes standalone ambient recording to internal storage, followed by syncing when connected to a phone. AI processing is an app-based workflow.

Are AI services included?+

Not yet confirmed. We are checking the software package, English-language experience, account requirements and any subscription or usage fees before offering it for sale.

Does it support call recording on my phone?+

The supplier shows a dedicated call mode. Compatibility with particular phones, cases and calling apps still needs testing.

Is it an OpenAI or ChatGPT device?+

No. This is a third-party supplier product. The seller uses AI platform names in its marketing; that does not establish an official partnership or included access.

When can I order in the US or Canada?+

This page is a collection preview. Retail price, final specification, delivery and warranty will be confirmed before orders open.
