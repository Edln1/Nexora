THE MI-1 GUIDE

Get to know every side.

Play modes

Automatic play

Movement for curious cats

Switch to automatic mode for moving play. Infrared sensors help detect obstacles. Use a clear floor area and supervise your cat.

Remote control

Take a turn together

Direct movement with the handheld transmitter. This is local remote control, not phone control over the internet.

Laser & feather

More ways to invite play

The feather attachment and built-in laser add variety. Avoid eyes and reflective surfaces, supervise play, and inspect attachments regularly.

Interaction

Voice commands

A few words. A response.

The MI-1 recognizes supported English and Chinese commands offline. Its documented wake phrase is “Hi WILL”, followed by a command such as “Go forward”. A custom MI-1 wake phrase is not confirmed.

Touch & expressions

Character you can interact with

Tap or stroke the head sensor for responses and sounds. Its expressions are interactive play features, not evidence of human-like feelings.

Growth stages

Character through play

The supplier describes four growth stages with different faces and responses as playtime accumulates. This is not confirmed personal memory or learning about your cat.

Product guide

Getting started

Make room for a first hello

Charge the robot and pair the supplied remote following the included manual. Try manual control in a clear, dry space before exploring automatic play.

Connectivity

Designed for offline play

The supplier lists a 433 MHz remote and offline voice commands. A phone app, camera recording, Wi-Fi control, open-ended AI chat and Synapses integration are not confirmed.

Care & play

A little attention goes a long way

Designed for interactive cat play. Keep the robot dry and supervise sessions. Stop if your cat appears uncomfortable. Follow the included manual for charging and replacing attachments.

Features refer to the underlying Wellturn MI-1 hardware. Consult the supplied manual before use.
