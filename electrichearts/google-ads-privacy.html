Google advertising privacy choices

With your permission, we load Google Ads measurement to understand which ads lead to visits and confirmed purchases. Google receives browser and device information, page URLs and conversion details: order value, currency and an opaque transaction identifier used to prevent duplicate counting.

We do not include your name, email, delivery address or Stripe checkout token in these events. Google may associate activity with browser or account identifiers. Declining does not affect checkout. Change your choice with the Google Ads cookie choices button. We respect Global Privacy Control.

Stripe payment records remain the source of truth. We only report completed, paid, live orders as purchases.
