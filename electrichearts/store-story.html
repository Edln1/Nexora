Nexora / Our story

Every story starts with one.

MI-1 is our first product launch. The 1 marks the beginning of Nexora.

Our first. Your new companion.

We’re starting with something small, expressive and playful. MI-1 brings movement and character to the moments you share with your cat—a new companion with a place in your family.

Technology with a place in everyday life.

Nexora is our home for discovering gadgets that feel interesting to use and easy to welcome into your day. MI-1 is our starting point. The rest of the collection is a look at what we’re exploring next.

Character, with clear expectations.

MI-1 is based on Wellturn MI-1 hardware. Its appeal is expressive eyes, offline voice commands and interactive cat play. We describe those features directly, without promising open-ended AI conversation or phone monitoring.
