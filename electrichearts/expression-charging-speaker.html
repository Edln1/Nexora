BEFORE IT JOINS YOUR DESK

Good questions.

Can I upload my own pixel art or use a Divoom app?

No custom-art app is documented for this N73 model. Its screen has built-in emoji effects, a clock and music-rhythm functions. It is a different product from Divoom’s Ditoo-Pro.

Does PIXO have a conversational AI assistant?

No voice AI or chat service is confirmed for this model. PIXO’s character comes from its display, touch reactions, sound and everyday functions.

Is the 15 W figure for the speaker?

No. The speaker is rated at 10 W. Up to 15 W is the advertised maximum wireless phone-charging output, which depends on phone compatibility and the power adapter.

Can I charge my phone when PIXO is unplugged?

The N73 has an internal battery for portable speaker use, but its wireless phone charger requires a suitable adapter plugged into PIXO. A wall adapter is not listed in the supplied package.
