01 / WITHIN REACH

A little conversation.
A different kind of watch.

For people drawn to playful technology, this is a different reason to wear a screen. Its appeal is the combination of a visible electronic core, a bright blue strap and a compact voice interface. It belongs to our companion collection; fitness tracking and phone replacement are not confirmed features.

02 / LOOK A LITTLE CLOSER

Small screen.
Plenty of character.

The listed 0.96-inch OLED sits beneath the speaker inside a transparent case. At 43 × 52 × 20 mm, the case is deliberately substantial enough to show the components. The 20 mm strap gives it a familiar watch format, while the exposed details make it feel distinctly its own.

03 / A CONNECTED COMPANION

Start with a voice.
Or a button.

The supplier lists voice wake-up and button wake-up interruption, giving two ways to begin interacting. Wi-Fi is part of the proposed setup. We are confirming the supported AI service, languages and account requirements before opening orders, so you can understand the complete experience before choosing.

A few things to know.

Can I order it now?

Not yet. This is a preview of our upcoming collection. Pricing and ordering will open after the launch details are confirmed.

Will it work in my country?

Language support, connected services and regional compatibility are being confirmed. We will publish these details before accepting orders.

Does it need a subscription?

Any account requirements, subscriptions or ongoing service charges will be disclosed before launch.

Where can I ask a question?

Contact Nexora and include the product name in your message.

Health sensors, activity tracking and phone notifications have not been confirmed for this model. The current preview focuses on its voice interface, OLED display and watch-style form.
