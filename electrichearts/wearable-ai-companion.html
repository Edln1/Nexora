01 / YOUR DAILY COMPANY

Make room for
a little company.

Some technology stays on the desk. This format is intended to come along. A compact body makes the device the focal point, with a simple silver-grey finish that fits comfortably alongside everyday belongings. The optional magnetic lanyard offers another way to keep it nearby.

02 / CONSIDERED FROM EVERY SIDE

Quiet design.
A personal presence.

Rounded edges, a dark front and a visible side control give the device a restrained appearance. It is the quieter member of the collection: less visually playful than our expressive-screen companions, with a design centered on carrying and wearing. Final dimensions and weight will be published before launch.

We are checking regional network support, language availability and ongoing charges before presenting them as part of a finished Nexora product.
